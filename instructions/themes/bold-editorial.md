# Bold editorial

## Position

Unapologetic confidence for products that need to be *noticed*: landing pages,
challenger brands, launches. Design as a statement of conviction, executed with
typographic discipline so it reads assured rather than loud.

## The one idea

**Scale contrast.** Headlines at genuinely oversized sizes against small, disciplined
supporting text — the jump *is* the brand. Everything else stays almost austere so the
scale can carry.

## Systems

- **Type:** one display face with real personality (tight grotesque, sharp slab — a face
  with opinions) plus a neutral text sans. Ratio ~1.6: 16 body, 26, 42, 68, 110 for the
  hero. Headlines tight (line-height ~1.05, slight negative tracking); body normal.
- **Space:** 8px base used asymmetrically — huge section gaps (128px+), tight internal
  groupings. Rhythm varies section to section; uniformity is the enemy here.
- **Color:** high contrast, near-black on near-white or inverted per section; **one**
  loud accent used at maybe three moments per page. Full-bleed color blocks over
  gradients. Duotone or hard-shadow treatment on imagery beats stock photos.
- **Surfaces:** radius 0; visible 2px borders where structure needs stating; hard offset
  shadows if any. Flat, printed, deliberate.
- **Motion:** few moves, biggest allowed: section reveals, a marquee, one scroll-driven
  moment. 300–400ms with sharp easing. Never parallax everything.

## Feels like / never

- Feels like: a poster you kept, a record sleeve, a manifesto.
- Never: the default dark-hero-purple-gradient look (`../anti-ai-tells.md` — this theme
  is the anti-template, which makes lapsing into template especially fatal), three-icon
  feature grids, glassmorphism, more than one accent, shouting *and* whispering nowhere.

## Fits

Marketing sites, launch pages, portfolios, brand-led products — content with a message.
Wrong voice for daily-use tools and data-dense apps: statement fatigue is real. Often
paired with a quieter theme for the product behind the landing page.

## Pairs with

a content-oriented static stack naturally; copy must match the register — assertive and
specific (`../copy.md`), because bold visuals over vague words collapse instantly.
