# Warm approachable

## Position

Friendly competence for people using something occasionally, maybe warily: lower the
intimidation, keep the credibility. For consumer tools and anything novices set up.

## The one idea

**Warmth through material, not mascots.** Temperature lives in the neutrals, the rounded
(not balloon-round) geometry, and forgiving feedback — never in cuteness, exclamation
marks, or emoji doing an icon's job.

## Systems

- **Type:** one humanist sans with genuine warmth (à la Nunito Sans / Karla territory —
  not a cold geometric). Body 16px minimum; scale ~1.3: 16/20/26/34. Headings in the
  heavier weight, never all-caps.
- **Space:** 8px base, roomy: touch targets 44px+, paddings one step more generous than
  feels efficient — this audience needs air, not density.
- **Color:** cream-warmed neutrals (never sterile grey-white); one friendly saturated
  accent plus a soft secondary; success used encouragingly. Errors stay softened in tone
  but perfectly clear in content (`../copy.md`). Dark mode warm and gentle, contrast
  still verified.
- **Surfaces:** radius 8–12px on interactive elements, one soft low shadow level, borders
  in tinted neutrals. Coherently soft — but every radius from the same scale, or it turns
  to mush.
- **Motion:** 200–250ms ease-out; small scale/fade acknowledgments on success. Motion
  reassures ("that worked"), never performs. Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`.

## Feels like / never

- Feels like: a good bank app, a well-designed utility your parent can use, patient help.
- Never: baby talk, mascots, confetti, gradient blobs, emoji as UI, TikTok energy —
  and never soft visuals hiding vague error messages. Friendly surface, precise words.

## Fits

Consumer apps, onboarding-heavy products, setup flows (`../ux-admin.md` checklists in
this voice), anything for non-experts. Wrong voice for pro tools daily-driven by experts
— warmth reads as slowness there.

## Pairs with

Any architecture; shines where `../ux-user.md`'s empty-state and form rules do the heavy
lifting.
